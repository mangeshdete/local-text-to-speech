Have you ever wondered why the ocean glows at night? It sounds like magic, but it's actually science. Tiny organisms called plankton light up whenever the water moves around them... and the effect is breathtaking! Waves crash, boats glide, and even a single footstep on the shore leaves a trail of blue sparks. Scientists believe this glow helps the plankton scare off predators. Clever, right? A quiet, glowing wonder that few people ever see. So the next time you visit a beach after dark, stay a little longer, look closely, and let the sea surprise you. Trust me, you won't forget it!
