A quiet street waits just before sunrise. A cyclist turns the corner, slows for the crosswalk, and looks toward the bakery. Inside, the first tray of bread hits the counter. The owner waves, and the cyclist smiles back. Is the coffee ready? Steam lifts off a cup by the window. Two students hurry past, sharing a pair of headphones. A bus door folds open, then shuts again. A dog sits by the gate, watching the whole thing without a sound. The cyclist pushes off, passes the green light, and disappears down the hill. The street is empty for a moment... then the bakery door swings, and the day begins!
